Drop the contents of the pdf.js prebuilt dist zip here.

After unzipping pdfjs-x.x.x-dist.zip, copy its contents into this folder
so that the path pdfjs/web/viewer.html exists.

Then update js/config.js:
  pdfViewerUrl: 'pdfjs/web/viewer.html',

Download from: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/releases
